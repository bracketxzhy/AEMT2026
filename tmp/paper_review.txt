Simulation and Results

Experimental Setup

Simulation experiments were conducted over a horizon of 500
decision steps in a bid to evaluate the long-term regulation
performance of the proposed method under random degradation.
At the beginning of each episode, the state variables were
initialized within admissible operating ranges around the
nominal equilibrium point, so that the system started from a
feasible but not perfectly balanced ecological condition. During
simulation, subsystem reliability decayed progressively
according to the degradation model introduced in Section 2,
while random disturbances were injected into the transition
dynamics to represent uncertainty in recovery efficiency, energy
availability, and environmental fluctuation [14].

The PPO policy was trained using the parameter settings
reported in the previous section. For evaluation, each
experiment was repeated over 50 independent runs, and all
reported results correspond to average performance across these

Beside improving short-term resource stability, the PPO-based
policy also reduced long-term envrionmental stress. Figure 3
shows that the ecological load grows more slowly under the
PPO-based policy than under the threshold-based controller.

Under the PPO-based reinforcement learning policy, ecological
load accumulation was reduced by approximately 38%
compared with the threshold-based controller. This
improvement indicates that the PPO policy was able to
coordinate resource recovery and energy expenditure more
effectively over time, instead of relying on short-term corrective
actions. In particular, the learned controller tended to suppress
deviation earlier, which limited the cumulative growth of
environmental burden across the episode.
